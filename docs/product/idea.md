Heading, idea, or maybe problem statement

Worklog is solving a problem where I recently, while updating my resume, was thinking: What to add to my resume that was impactful and that would make an impression on the reviewer?

Luckily, as my mentor said one time, "Create all the documents of what you did at your internship." I created that doc, and it was put in Notion. What I really found out was that the doc I created was post my internship period, and I tried to recall things. Maybe I have forgotten something or I have not noted something.

The main problem I'm trying to solve here is, somehow, to log everything. It could be professional, things happening at work, emotional things, office politics, or actual work they do. Everything, but in an organised way.

Okay, let's redefine the problem statement again. Right now, there is no such tool that tells working professionals to log their day-to-day things happening at work. This will help them keep a trail of the work they are doing and the things happening, which will be helpful mainly to get a proper timeline of their work, how they did it, whether that was impactful or not, etc.

That is the problem, and I think the best solution is to prepare an AI agent like Claude itself, with a similar kind of UI, because most people are using Claude. Professional people are using Claude, not only in coding but in other fields as well. That is my audience. Every working professional, regardless of their seniority, is my audience, and I want them to use my app.

I want to create an interface like Claude where they can just come and talk through their day, and everything is managed by my agent. The agent can ask follow-up questions regarding any confusion, maybe, but mostly, that AI agent will be a working professional companion or a logger. Basically, the main job of that AI agent is to log everything and organise whatever the guy is saying to the agent.

Okay, now what I look into is V1. V1 will have the primary interaction with the agent in context, and people can come up and say anything they want, maybe about what happened in a week, month, or that specific day.

This will be the job of the AI agent: to decipher things and manage everything in an organisation, and to ask whenever there is any confusion regarding the date or the timeline, and get it reviewed by the person who is responsible.

The next feature I want is that there should be a feature to have separation between projects. Let's say I am contributing to open source, and I am working professionally, and I am working on a personal project. What I can do is create different projects, but I still will have a single interface to chat with my developer agent. The AI agent will decide what thing goes where. Finally, before saving everything, we'll take a review from the user about whether this log is correct and going into the correct project or not.

The technical things which this project will be built on will be:

- Node.js backend
- Postgres database
- Frontend will be on Next.js
- For CSS, we'll use Tailwind
- We'll use Shadcn UI
